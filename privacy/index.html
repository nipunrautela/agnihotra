Your information, explained

This policy covers the Agnihotra Android app provided by Nipun Rautela. For privacy questions or requests, email rautelanipun@gmail.com.

Places, preferences, and calculations

Saved place names, coordinates, time zones, approximate area names, your selected/home place, and preferences are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Sunrise and sunset calculations happen on the device. We do not operate an account or a server that synchronizes your saved places. App-managed cloud backup and device transfer are disabled.

Intended audience

Agnihotra is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not ask for a birth date or collect an age category. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the app, please contact us at rautelanipun@gmail.com.

Location and online lookup

With your Android location permission, the app can obtain a current location during setup and check distance from home while the app is open. Travel prompts use about 25 km, accounting for reported accuracy. The app does not request background location access.

When you use current location, or open a saved current-location place that has no area name yet, coordinates may be sent to Android’s geocoding provider to suggest a neighbourhood or city subtitle. Explicit city/address searches may send the search text to that provider. The provider and its processing depend on the device, and lookups may need the internet. Travel distance calculations do not use geocoding. Manual entry, bundled city choices, and calculations for saved places work without that service.

Optional usage analytics

Google’s Firebase Analytics is enabled by default to help us understand app usage and improve setup and reliability. You can turn it off in Settings without losing app features. When enabled, it processes app-instance identifiers, device/app information, automatic session and lifecycle events, and selected app interactions. Approximate city, region, and country reporting is derived from the internet connection’s IP address; it is not the saved practice location and can be inaccurate. This processing is not fully anonymous.

Our parameter-free custom events measure location setup, settings visits, reminder settings and notification permission, timetable use and export outcomes, widget setup, and chime previews. They do not include saved coordinates, place or neighbourhood names, search text, birth dates, age categories, mantra activity, or practice completion. We do not set an account User-ID. Advertising ID collection, ad personalization, and automatic screen reporting are disabled in the app. The app contains no ads, purchases, or crash-reporting SDK.

You can turn Usage analytics off in Settings without losing core functionality. This stops future collection; it does not delete analytics previously received by Google. Firebase transmits analytics using TLS. See Google’s privacy policy and Google Analytics privacy information.

Sharing that you request

A monthly CSV export contains the selected place name, coordinates, time zone, dates, and event times. Daily text sharing includes the place name, date, event times, and time zone. You choose the destination using Android’s document picker or share chooser. That app or storage service handles the information under its own policies. Copying a mantra places its text on Android’s clipboard. Widgets display the selected place and its area subtitle on your home screen.

Retention and deletion

Local places and preferences remain until you delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall. Exported files and shared messages must be deleted separately. The Google Analytics property is configured for 2-month event-data retention and 14-month user-data retention, with reset on new user activity off. Google applies its expiration/deletion processing; standard aggregated reports may remain longer.

For a privacy or deletion request, contact rautelanipun@gmail.com. We do not have an account directory that identifies an analytics installation by name or email, so identifying a particular record may require further information. Do not send precise coordinates or sensitive practice information. Opting out, clearing storage, or uninstalling does not guarantee removal of previously transmitted analytics.

Permissions and security

Notification and exact-alarm access support reminders; vibration is used when enabled. Location access is optional. Exports use the Android picker instead of broad storage access. Android’s sandbox and device protections protect local app files; the app does not claim a separately encrypted database. No device or service can guarantee absolute security. We do not sell your personal data.

This page and other services

Opening external references, email, or policy links uses another app or website and its own privacy practices. This policy page has no analytics or advertising scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses for security under the GitHub privacy statement.

Updates

We will update this page and its effective date when the app’s data practices change. Contact: rautelanipun@gmail.com.

वैकल्पिक उपयोग विश्लेषण

Google की Firebase Analytics सेवा शुरू में चालू रहती है और सेटिंग्स से बंद कर सकते हैं। चालू होने पर यह सेवा ऐप-इंस्टेंस पहचानकर्ता, ऐप/डिवाइस की जानकारी, अपने-आप बनने वाले सत्र इवेंट और कुछ ऐप गतिविधियाँ प्राप्त करती है। कनेक्शन के IP पते से अनुमानित शहर, क्षेत्र और देश मिलते हैं। यह अभ्यास का सहेजा हुआ स्थान नहीं है और अनुमान गलत हो सकता है। जानकारी पूरी तरह गुमनाम नहीं है।

हमारे बिना पैरामीटर वाले कस्टम इवेंट स्थान सेटअप, सेटिंग्स खोलना, रिमाइंडर सेटिंग/सूचना अनुमति, समय-सारणी और निर्यात के परिणाम, विजेट सेटअप और ध्वनि का पूर्वावलोकन मापते हैं। इनमें निर्देशांक, स्थान/क्षेत्र के नाम, खोज-पाठ, जन्म तिथि, आयु वर्ग, मंत्र गतिविधि या अभ्यास पूरा होने की जानकारी नहीं होती। खाते का User-ID नहीं दिया जाता। ऐप में विज्ञापन पहचानकर्ता का संग्रह, विज्ञापन वैयक्तिकरण और अपने-आप स्क्रीन रिपोर्ट करना बंद है। विज्ञापन, खरीद और क्रैश रिपोर्टिंग SDK नहीं हैं।

सेटिंग्स में उपयोग विश्लेषण बंद कर सकते हैं; मुख्य सुविधाएँ उपलब्ध रहती हैं। इससे आगे का संग्रह रुकता है, Google को पहले मिला डेटा नहीं मिटता। Firebase विश्लेषण TLS से भेजता है। अधिक जानकारी: Google की निजता नीति और Google Analytics की निजता जानकारी।
