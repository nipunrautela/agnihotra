# Agnihotra

A native Android app for location-based sunrise and sunset times, daily
reminders, and Agnihotra mantra resources. Designed for India, with English
and Hindi support and a modern, accessible interface.

## Features

- Sunrise and sunset times calculated on the device, with a countdown to the
  next event and offline access for saved locations.
- Current-location detection or manual entry, a home location, and
  multiple saved places. Current-location places show an approximate area subtitle
  when a name is available.
- A travel prompt when opening the app at least 25 km from home.
- Preparation reminders and event alarms with a warm two-note chime and vibration,
  a five-minute default duration, and a configurable 1–30 minute limit. Stop a
  ringing alarm from its notification.
- A focus countdown, optional seconds, and 12/24-hour time formats.
- Home-screen widgets that follow the selected place or stay on a saved place.
- A monthly timetable with date selection, daily sharing, and CSV export.
- Offline city suggestions and editable coordinates for location setup.
- Mantra text with transliteration, larger text, and copy controls. Audio
  recordings are planned for a later update.
- Light and dark themes inspired by warm ivory, copper, amber, and dusk.

## Availability and privacy

Agnihotra is being developed for ages 13 and older on Android 8.0 and newer.
It will be free and ad-free, with no account required.

Analytics is enabled by default, with opt-out in settings. It includes
app-instance identifiers and approximate city-level reporting from the connection.
Advertising features are disabled. Custom events exclude saved locations and
mantra activity.

Saved places and settings stay on the device. Location access is used while the
app is open. Online search and area-name lookup
may send coordinates or search text to the device’s geocoding
provider. Offline city suggestions and manual entry are available to everyone.
Timetable files are exported or shared only when requested.
